I am an EPSRC Postdoctoral Pathway Fellow at Imperial College London, developing an independent research programme in combinatorial neural algorithmic reasoning for structured decision-making.

I recently completed my PhD at Imperial, specialising in computational optimisation and machine learning, under the supervision of [Dr Maria Papathanasiou](https://www.papathanlab.com/){: style="color: #009999;"} in the [Sargent Centre for Process Systems Engineering](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/process-systems-engineering/){: style="color: #009999;"}.

My research combines **machine learning**, **mathematical optimisation** and **control** to develop optimisation-compatible learning methods that are scalable, reliable and interpretable. My work includes:

<ul>
  <li>Learning energy functions and neural representations for combinatorial optimisation with constrained inference</li>
  <li>Integrating differentiable and mixed integer optimisation into learning systems</li>
  <li>Developing reduced-space and multi-fidelity Bayesian optimisation methods for expensive, constrained black-box problems</li>
</ul>

I apply these methods to structured decision-making problems in personalised cell and gene therapies, energy systems and large-scale logistics.

During my PhD, I worked as an **Applied Scientist Intern** at <a href="https://www.amazon.science/" style="color: #009999;">Amazon</a> on large-scale fulfilment optimisation.
